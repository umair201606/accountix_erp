13 weekly columns, Mon–Sun. Rolling the start date forward re-buckets every auto and recurring line — that is how the forecast rolls.

How this forecast works
  • Opening cash — the actual cash balance just before the window starts, straight from your general ledger.
  • Auto rows — open customer and supplier balances are aged and spread across the weeks (0–30 days → weeks 1–2, 31–60 → weeks 3–5, 61–90 → weeks 6–8, older → weeks 9–13). No entry needed.
  • Your lines — anything the ledger can't see yet: payroll, rent, taxes, loan repayments, one-off items. Add one below; it lands in the week of its date and repeats if you pick a frequency.
    • {% if matrix.has_payroll_auto %}
  • Salaries & wages are forecast automatically from your approved HR payroll runs — add a payroll line only if you pay something extra.
    • {% endif %}
  • Finished weeks — replaced with actual cash movement; the variance row shows whether the forecast held. Set a minimum cash floor in Finance Settings → Cash flow to add a headroom check.
