Why Accountix

The parts of an ERP that usually drift apart are held together here by design.

Company data stays separated

Every scoped query is filtered by the active company at the engine level, not by hand in each route — so a missing filter fails closed instead of leaking rows.

Stock value equals ledger value

Cost lives in stock layers, and layer value is reconciled against the ledger's running cost. Valuation and the balance sheet cannot quietly disagree.

Rights that follow the person

Module access and section rights are set per company. Move someone between companies and their access moves with the role, not the login.

Reports you can hand over

Screen, PDF and spreadsheet render the same figures through the same formatter — an export never disagrees with what you just read.
