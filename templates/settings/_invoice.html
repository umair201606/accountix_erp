Tax Application Defaults

Document-wide tax defaults and the over-invoicing tolerance (§11.2). Every order and invoice inherits these and may override them per document.

Apply further tax defaults to Off, and turns on automatically for unregistered customers.
Apply withholding is defaulted per partner class on the document itself.
Used by §4.4 — how far an invoice may exceed the quantities/values of the orders it is created from before the save is blocked.

Default Calculation Scopes

Whether discount, charges and tax are entered once for the whole document (Combined) or line by line (Per item). Documents may switch scope unless the matching per-line toggle below is hidden.

Form-field Visibility

Controls which fields and toggles appear on the day-to-day creation form (§11.3). Hiding a control removes it entirely and forces its behaviour — most businesses use only a subset, and hiding the rest keeps data-entry staff from options that don't apply.

Charge-ledger Defaults

Seed values applied when a ledger is picked in the Additional Charges form (§6.1). Every one of them stays overridable per charge, per document.

ST / W-H / Extra are the default tax-base inclusions for a charge when it is billed. Further tax has no switch of its own — applied to a document, it is always computed on the sales-tax base. Absorbed and expense-only ledgers have no base switches at all: an absorbed amount sits inside the line values and is therefore always taxed with the goods, and an expense-only amount never reaches the counterparty.
