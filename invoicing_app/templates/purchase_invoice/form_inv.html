Additional Charges

Each charge picks a ledger account, a scope (Combined / Per item), a treatment, and its tax bases. Bill = the supplier's own charge line (adds to what we owe). Absorb = carriage inward, capitalised into item cost. Expense = we bear it (posts to the expense ledger). ST / WHT switch each billed charge into the input-sales-tax and withholding bases.

Import from Purchase Orders

Tick one or more open purchase orders. Each line's quantity is capped at what is still uninvoiced; fully-invoiced orders are shown greyed and cannot be picked.

Select a supplier first, then click "From Orders"
