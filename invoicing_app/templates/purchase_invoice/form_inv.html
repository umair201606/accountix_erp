Charges this invoice

Add freight, installation or any other charge, then choose who pays for it and how it is taxed.

A purchase carries no further tax — it is a sales-side levy (§8).

Treatment — 3 options

1 · Supplier bills it

The supplier's own charge line — it adds to what we owe. Its switches decide whether it enters the input-tax / W-H / extra tax base.

2 · Absorb into item cost

Carriage inward: spread pro-rata into the line Amounts and capitalised into inventory as landed cost. No separate line prints; it is inside every tax base automatically.

3 · Expense ledger only

Not on the supplier's invoice at all. Posts as Dr expense ledger / Cr charges payable alongside the invoice. Tax-base switches are disabled.

Every charge saved here is listed by name in the totals panel, directly below the Input sales tax line, with badges for its scope and treatment.
