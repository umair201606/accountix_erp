Charges this invoice

Add freight, installation or any other charge, then choose who pays for it and how it is taxed.

Further tax has no switch of its own — when applied to the invoice, it is always computed on the sales-tax base.

Treatment — 3 options

1 · Bill to customer

Charge prints on the invoice and adds to net payable. Its switches decide whether it enters the sales-tax / W-H / extra tax base; the further-tax base always equals the sales-tax base.

2 · Absorb into item values

Charge is spread pro-rata into the line Amounts (carriage-inward style). No separate line prints; it is inside every tax base automatically.

3 · Expense ledger only

Customer is not billed. Amount posts as Dr expense ledger / Cr charges payable alongside the invoice. Tax-base switches are disabled.

Every charge saved here is listed by name in the invoice totals panel, directly below the Sales tax line, with badges for its scope and treatment.
